# Skybridge at Kingdom Tower

Enjoy breathtaking views at the heights of one of Riyadh’s most iconic landmarks.  
The Skybridge at Kingdom Tower offers visitors an unforgettable journey above the city of **Riyadh, Saudi Arabia**, combining architectural excellence with a premium digital experience.

This project focuses on designing a seamless and engaging digital presence that allows users to explore the Skybridge, reserve their seats, and shop for exclusive products related to the experience.

## Project Overview
- Project Type: Mobile Application & Website
- Location: Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
- Industry: Tourism / Landmark Experience
- Target Audience: Local and international visitors
- Focus: UI/UX Design for booking, exploration, and shopping experiences

##  Mobile & Web Experience
The mobile application and website were designed to:
- Showcase the Skybridge experience through immersive visuals
- Enable smooth and intuitive seat reservation
- Provide essential visitor information
- Offer an integrated shopping section for related products
- Deliver a premium experience aligned with the identity of a major Saudi landmark

##  Platform Features
- Experience overview & highlights
- Seat reservation & booking flow
- Integrated store 
- Visitor information & guidance
- Responsive design across devices

## Store Section
The store section allows visitors to explore and purchase curated items related to the Skybridge experience, enhancing engagement and extending the visit beyond the physical journey.

##  Dashboard
The platform includes an administrative dashboard intended for operational and content management.  
Note: The dashboard implementation was outside the scope of my contribution to this project.
